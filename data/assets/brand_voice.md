# Excalidraw brand voice brief (zh-CN)

Excalidraw's English UI is casual, friendly and a little playful, matching its
hand-drawn look ("Hold your horses, you're too fast for us!"). It talks to the
user plainly, never in legal or corporate tone, except where it has to
(licence, privacy).

For zh-CN transcreation (tier 4):
- Write for the moment, not word for word: an idiom becomes a natural Chinese
  equivalent, not a literal one.
- Friendly and light, addressed as 你 is acceptable on playful lines; keep 您 in
  error and data-loss messages for consistency with the rest of the UI.
- Upsell lines say what the user gets (more usage, a free trial) in one short
  sentence; no hard sell.
- Product names stay in English: Excalidraw, Excalidraw+.

Example lines (source → direction, not final copy):
- "Hold your horses, you're too fast for us!" → a light, natural "slow down" line, not 稳住你的马.
- "Try out Excalidraw+ for more" → make clear that "more" means more AI usage.
